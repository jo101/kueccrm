# 10: Day-one checklist for a new BD team member

Complete this on your first day, with the ops coordinator nearby. It takes about 30 minutes. Read `10-cadence-en.md` (or `10-cadence-ar.md`) first.

**Before the new person starts, the ED** invites their work address with the **BD officer** role (`runbooks/13-roles.md` section 7; the ops coordinator has no Members permission). **Then the ops coordinator:**

- creates a work package titled `TEST - <first name>`, owned by the new person, with a due date 3 working days away, any short done criteria, and status **Offered**;
- creates an opportunity titled `TEST - <first name>`, owned by the new person, with **Type** left empty (setting a type starts a real playbook and creates real work).

## The five questions

Tick each one when you can answer yes.

| # | Question | How | Yes when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Can you log in? | Open the CRM address IT gave you and choose **Continue with Microsoft** with your KUEC address. | You see the sidebar with **Home** and **My work**. |
| 2 | Can you find your own work? | Click **My work** in the sidebar. | `TEST - <your first name>` is in the list, with status Offered. |
| 3 | Can you accept a work package? | Open the test work package. On the card, press **Accept**. | The status reads **Accepted** and the card now shows **Request help** and **Submit**. |
| 4 | Can you set a next action on an opportunity? | Open **Pipeline**, find your test opportunity, and fill in **Next action** ("Call the client to confirm the meeting") and **Next action date** (tomorrow). | Both fields are saved, and the opportunity no longer appears on the **Exceptions** page under No next action. |
| 5 | Can you ask for help? | On the test work package card, press **Request help** and type one line, for example "Testing: please confirm you received this." | The ops coordinator confirms they received a task called "Help needed: TEST - <your first name>". |

## Then connect your mailbox

With the ops coordinator, connect your Microsoft mail and calendar (Settings > Accounts > Emails > **Connect with Microsoft**) with the team's settings: email visibility **Subject and metadata**, contact auto-creation **None**, calendar visibility **Everything**, calendar auto-creation off (`config/dashboards/email-calendar-sync.md`). Then read "How to find anything" in `runbooks/12-global-features.md`, and open **Help** at the bottom of the sidebar: it has this checklist, the cadence guide, what every list and field means and a short video for each screen. Not sure what to do with a work package? Press **Request help** on its card.
