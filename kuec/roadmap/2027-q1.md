## 2. Every request or gap, in one bucket each

Rules for placing an item:

- **Configure** (do this week): a change to something that already exists: a view, a field, a select option, a role grant, a rule constant, a fix or small extension of an existing logic function, a runbook or IT line. "Workflow" here means app logic: `kuec/CLAUDE.md` section 6 rules out UI workflows. Rows marked **GO** block go-live and go first; the rest ship in the weekly releases before January.
- **Phase B component** (SDK app, next sprint): a new front component, route or logic function in `kuec-bd-ops`, built in `design/backlog.md` order.
- **Execution service**: needs a system that owns case execution, beyond what record state and an hourly sweep can hold. A change to today's timer thresholds or recipients is configure; a new timer on stored dates is Phase B.
- **Enterprise licence**: needs Twenty's Organization plan.
- **Never**: out of scope, with the reason.

Days are one engineer's days from `design/backlog.md` where it gives them; "est." marks an estimate made for this roadmap.

## 4. uVENTURE boundary check

Checked: every object, field, view, logic function and role in `kuec/app/src`, and both template files in `kuec/playbooks`, for the five record kinds uVENTURE owns and their attributes (TRL, IP status, inventors, patent numbers, licence terms).

| uVENTURE record | In Twenty | Duplicate? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technology | Nothing. A licence case opens "carrying the technology's uVENTURE record link" (`templates.json`), but the field does not exist | No. A missing link: C4 |
| Patent | Nothing. "Record the licence against the patent" is a uVENTURE hand-off for a person | No |
| Equipment | Nothing | No |
| Industry challenge | Nothing today. B8 adds an Industry challenge case type that carries the challenge's link | No, as long as B8 copies nothing but the link |
| Licence | **Contract, kind Licence.** W7 creates one, status Signed and Signed on today, whenever a licence opportunity is signed (`w07-opportunity-signed.ts`). The Licence signed step's done criteria ask for counterparty, type, status, key dates and value in Twenty and the uVENTURE records updated (`templates.json`, Licence signed). Signed contract value this year and Contracts expiring in 90 days on BD > Executive, and Hygiene: past expiry, still Signed, all read that copy | **Yes.** One licence, keyed by hand in two systems |

The technical assessment's TRL and IP status go in a note on SharePoint, not in Twenty, so they pass. A Capability pointer object (integration map 3.2) would duplicate technologies and equipment; it is not built and stays N1.

**Why it happened.** `kuec/CLAUDE.md` section 4 lets Twenty hold contract metadata (counterparty, type, status, key dates, value, owner). Section 5 says licences live in uVENTURE and Twenty never copies their attributes. For a licence the two rules collide, and the playbook settled it with double entry.

**The fix (C5, about 1 day, the ED's yes first).** Section 5 wins for licences. Section 4 still covers Service and NDA contracts, which uVENTURE does not hold.

1. W7 stops creating a contract for a signed licence opportunity. It creates one Task for the case owner instead: "Record the licence in uVENTURE against the technology on this case", deduped like every other notification.
2. The Contract kind option Licence is relabelled `Licence (deprecated)`, with the date and reason in a comment, and taken out of every view and filter. It is not deleted (`kuec/CLAUDE.md` section 2).
3. The Licence signed done criteria become: executed agreement on SharePoint, licence recorded in uVENTURE against the technology, and against the patent if it covers one. Then `render-playbooks.ts` and `yarn playbooks:sync`.
4. Licence counts already come from opportunities (**BD: Signed licences this year** is an Opportunity view), so they do not change. **Signed contract value this year** and **Contracts expiring in 90 days** stop including licences; licence value and renewals are reported from uVENTURE.
5. Tests: W7 on a signed licence opportunity creates no contract and one Task; `views.test.ts` fails if a view filters on the Licence kind.
6. Nothing to migrate: production holds no records yet. Licence contracts on staging are test records and are deleted.

Ask the uVENTURE administrator whether option agreements are recorded there too. If they are, Option follows Licence through the same steps.

One rule to hold from now on: researchers and faculty may be People, as contacts. Inventorship (an "inventor of" field or relation) is a uVENTURE attribute and never goes on a Person.

## 6. For the CEO

**What the system does.** It is proven on a test build of Twenty 2.48.0. Production waits for the 2.48.0 release and four small fixes, 2 to 3 engineering days in all.

- Gives each licence, RFQ or workshop case its plan: steps with owners, reviewers and due dates in UAE working days.
- Chases on its own. Work not accepted in 2 working days goes to the owner and the ED, and to the ops coordinator at 5. Overdue work goes to the owner on the day and to the ED 2 days later. Every block reaches the ED with its reason. Every morning at 08:00, an open case without a next step goes back to its owner.
- Puts what it does not chase on one Data quality page, cleared every Monday in 15 minutes.
- Limits who can change what, through 11 roles. BD officers cannot move due dates or reassign work. Leadership has read-only access.
- Restores from backup: all 124 tables came back identical in the test.
