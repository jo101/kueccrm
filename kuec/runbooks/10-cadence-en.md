# 10: How the BD team works, day to day

For everyone on the BD team, including people new to business development. Arabic version: `10-cadence-ar.md`. Day-one checklist: `10-onboarding.md`. Screen names in **bold** are exactly what you see in the CRM.

## 1. The three rules

1. **Every opportunity has an owner, a next action and a date.** If you own it, you keep all three filled in, with the date today or later. Every morning at 08:00 the CRM gives the owner a task for each open opportunity missing one of them.
2. **Accept a work package within 2 working days, or say why not.** Working days are Monday to Friday, without public holidays. After 2 days the CRM reminds you and tells the ED. After 5 days the work is taken from you and given to the ops coordinator.
3. **Blocked means you say what is blocking you. Never silence.** Set the work package to **Blocked** and write the reason. The ED is told straight away, with your reason, and gets a task to decide.

And one rule for everyone at KUEC: **a contact that is not a Lead in the CRM did not happen, and whoever logged it holds the referral credit.** Met someone with a possible need? Create a **Lead** the same working day, even with only a name and a company. How leads are triaged, qualified and converted: `19-leads.md`.

## 2. Daily standup: 10 minutes

Everyone opens **My work** in the sidebar and reads from it. Each person takes about 2 minutes and says three things:

- "Yesterday I finished ..." (name the work package or opportunity).
- "Today I will ..." (the next item on My work, earliest due date first).
- "I am blocked by ..." or "Nothing is blocking me."

No problem-solving during the round. If something is blocked, it must already show **Blocked** in the CRM; if not, set it before the standup ends. Discussions happen afterwards, with only the people who need to be there.

## 3. Thursday review: 30 minutes

Everyone on the team, with the ED. Fix records in the CRM during the meeting, so the screen matches what was agreed.

| Time | Item | Where | Leave with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 min | Weekly dashboard | **BD Executive** in the sidebar | What closed this week, what moved, what is stuck |
| 5 min | No next action | **Exceptions** page | Every row has a next action and a date |
| 5 min | Overdue | **Exceptions** page | Every row has a new due date or is finished |
| 5 min | Blocked | **Exceptions** page | Each block has a person and a day to clear it |

## 4. What "done" means for the six most common work packages

Submit only when all three lines are true. Documents go on SharePoint with a link in the work package; never upload them to the CRM.

**Technical assessment**
- A short note covering how ready the technology is, its patent position, the development still needed, and whether the researcher will stay involved.
- The researcher has confirmed the facts in the note.
- The ED has signed it off.

**Route scoping** (how we take a technology to market)
- One recommended route (a licence, an option to license later, or joint development) with the reasons.
- Target industries named, with the likely deal type and a rough value range.
- The ED's go or no-go decision is recorded on the work package.

**Partner shortlist**
- A ranked list of companies most likely to take the technology, each one entered in the CRM.
- Each company has at least one named contact and a sentence or two on why it fits.
- The reviewer has approved the list.

**Proposal** (or quote)
- Every requirement in the client's request is answered.
- The price is built from the estimated effort, and finance has reviewed it.
- The document is on SharePoint and linked from the work package.

**Workshop audience list**
- Named people in the roles CEO, CTO, head of R&D and innovation manager, each entered in the CRM and linked to their company.
- At least 30 invitees for every 10 people we expect to attend.
- The reviewer has approved the list.

**Workshop outcomes**
- Every attendee who asked for a follow-up or described a problem has an opportunity with an owner, a next action and a date, or a "no fit" note.
- The number of interested people and of opportunities created is recorded on the project, even if it is zero.
- Each industry problem raised is recorded in uVENTURE and linked from its opportunity.

## 5. The Work package card

Open a work package from **My work** and the card is on its first tab. From a list, select the row and run **Open work package card**. Only the owner sees the buttons. Each button has an info icon beside it: hover it, or press Tab, to read what the button does. The last line of the card, **What do I do now?**, names your next step.

| Button | When you see it | What it means | What happens next |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Accept** | The work is offered to you | "This is mine. I will deliver it by the due date." | Reminders stop. If it is not finished by the due date, you are told that day and the ED 2 working days later. |
| **Decline** | The work is offered to you | "This should not be mine," with a one-line reason. | It leaves your list. The ops coordinator reads the reason and gives it to someone else. |
| **Request help** | Whenever the work is yours and not finished: Planned, Offered, Accepted, Submitted or Blocked | "I am stuck or unsure. I need someone to talk it through." | The ops coordinator gets a task, due today at 17:00, linked to your work package. It stays yours and keeps its status. |
| **Submit** | After you accept | "It is finished and meets its done criteria." | The reviewer is told. They mark it **Done**, which offers the next step to its owner, or send it back to you with what is missing. |

**Blocked** is not on the card. Use **Open full record**, set Status to **Blocked** and fill in **Blocked reason** in the same edit. When the block clears, set the status back to **Accepted**.

## 6. When you do not know what to do

Press **Request help** on the card. Do it the same day you get stuck. Waiting quietly is the one thing that always costs more time than asking. Every page that shows a work package says so in its **How this record works** or **How this tab works** box, and so does **Help** in the sidebar.

- Not accepted yet? Press **Request help** before you decide, or accept it first if it is meant for you. If you think it should go to someone else, **Decline** and say why.
- Blocked and not sure how to clear it? **Request help** stays on the card while the work is blocked.
- Stuck on an opportunity? Set its next action to "Ask for help on ..." with today's date, and raise it at the standup.
- Someone else needs to act before you can continue? That is not "help", it is a block: set **Blocked** with the reason.
